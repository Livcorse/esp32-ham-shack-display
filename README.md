# DX Live Panel by YO2CR

**Cluster and DXpeditions in real time** (formerly "Shack Panel").

A stand-alone WiFi shack display for the **Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-7** (800x480):
UTC and local clock, weather with a 7-day forecast, band conditions, DX cluster spots
(up to 3 clusters) marking the countries you still need, active and upcoming DXpeditions,
alerts, night mode, English / Romanian.

Needed and unconfirmed countries come from your logbooks: **QRZ.com, ARRL LoTW, Club Log, eQSL**
(red = never worked, orange = worked but not confirmed, purple = needed on that band).
The "Heard near me" filter shows only spots reported within 500 / 1000 km of you.
Optional **phone notifications** (free ntfy app, no account) for a NEW ONE, wherever you are.

With the optional PC agent (Windows): your radio's frequency on the panel and
"tap a spot -> the radio goes there" (Yaesu, Icom, Kenwood, Elecraft, Xiegu ... via Hamlib).

## Download

Go to **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** and download `Shack_Panel_YO2CR_kit_EN_....zip`.
Inside: `GUIDE_EN.txt` (step by step setup), the panel firmware and the PC agent.

## Updates

The panel checks this page once a day. When a new version is out it tells you on the screen:
SETUP > tap the version line at the top > **Update now**. Your settings are kept.
